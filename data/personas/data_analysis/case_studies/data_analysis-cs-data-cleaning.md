# CRM data cleaning and deduplication

**Client:** a B2B software reseller  
**Industry:** Software  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem
The CRM had 40k contacts with duplicates and broken company names.

## What I did
Built fuzzy-matching deduplication with a review queue for uncertain matches.

## Outcome
Removed 9,300 duplicates at 98% precision on a manual audit sample.
