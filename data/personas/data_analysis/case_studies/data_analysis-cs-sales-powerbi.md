# Sales performance dashboard

**Client:** a FMCG distributor in Karachi  
**Industry:** FMCG  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem
Regional managers waited until the 10th of each month for an Excel sales pack.

## What I did
Modelled sales, targets and distributor data into a star schema and built a Power BI dashboard refreshed daily.

## Outcome
Monthly reporting time went from 3 days to 0; 45 managers use it weekly.

**Skills:** Microsoft Power BI, Dashboard, SQL, Data Modeling, Business Intelligence
