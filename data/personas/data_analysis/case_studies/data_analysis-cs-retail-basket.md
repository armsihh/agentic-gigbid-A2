# Market basket analysis

**Client:** a supermarket chain  
**Industry:** Retail  
**Year:** 2022

## The problem
Promotions were planned without knowing which products were bought together.

## What I did
Ran association-rule mining on 2M receipts and recommended cross-promotions and shelf placements.

## Outcome
Two recommended bundles lifted category sales 6% in the pilot stores.
