# Marketing channel attribution

**Client:** a US DTC supplements brand  
**Industry:** E-commerce  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem
The brand spent $80k/month on ads with no view of which channel drove repeat buyers.

## What I did
Built GA4 export pipelines into BigQuery, transformation models for sessions and orders, and a multi-touch attribution report.

## Outcome
Reallocated 22% of budget from paid social to search; blended CAC down 17%.
