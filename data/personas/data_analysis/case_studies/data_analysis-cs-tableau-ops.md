# Operations KPI dashboard in Tableau

**Client:** a hospital network  
**Industry:** Healthcare  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem
Bed occupancy and ER wait times were reported weekly from different systems.

## What I did
Built a Tableau dashboard joining admissions, bed and ER data with drill-down by ward.

## Outcome
Leadership reviews it daily; ER wait time breaches surfaced 2 days earlier on average.

**Skills:** Tableau, Dashboard, SQL, Data Visualization
