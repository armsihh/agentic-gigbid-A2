# Employee attrition analysis

**Client:** a call centre with 1,200 staff  
**Industry:** BPO  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem
Attrition was 60% a year and HR did not know which teams or shifts drove it.

## What I did
Ran survival analysis on tenure by shift, manager and pay band, and presented the drivers to leadership.

## Outcome
Night-shift pay change based on the analysis cut attrition in that group by 14 points.

**Skills:** Data Analysis, Statistics, Python, Data Visualization
