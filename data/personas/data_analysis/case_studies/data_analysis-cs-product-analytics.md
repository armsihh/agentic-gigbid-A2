# Product usage funnel analysis

**Client:** a mobile fitness app  
**Industry:** Health & Fitness  
**Year:** 2025

## The problem
The app lost 70% of users before the first workout and nobody knew why.

## What I did
Built event-level funnels and cohorts, found the drop at the permissions screen, and analysed the fix experiment.

## Outcome
Day-1 activation rose from 30% to 41% after the onboarding change.

**Skills:** Analytics, SQL, Data Visualization, A/B Testing
