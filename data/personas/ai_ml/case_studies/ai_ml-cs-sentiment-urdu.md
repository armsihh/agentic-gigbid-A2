# Roman-Urdu sentiment analysis

**Client:** a Pakistani telecom's social media team  
**Industry:** Telecom  
**Year:** 2022

## The problem
English sentiment tools failed on Roman-Urdu complaints on social media.

## What I did
Labelled 12k posts with the client, trained a BiLSTM and a fine-tuned multilingual transformer, and built a daily dashboard feed.

## Outcome
Macro-F1 of 0.82 versus 0.51 for the off-the-shelf tool.

**Skills:** Natural Language Processing, Machine Learning, TensorFlow, Python
