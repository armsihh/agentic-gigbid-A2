# Customer churn prediction

**Client:** a regional telecom operator  
**Industry:** Telecom  
**Year:** 2022

## The problem
The retention team called random customers and had no idea who was about to leave.

## What I did
Engineered 60 usage features from billing SQL tables, compared logistic regression, random forest and gradient boosting, and explained predictions per customer.

## Outcome
Top-decile lift of 3.4x; the retention campaign saved an estimated 1,100 subscribers in one quarter.

**Skills:** Machine Learning, Python Scikit-Learn, Data Science, Statistics, SQL
