# Demand forecasting for a pharmacy chain

**Client:** a pharmacy chain with 40 branches  
**Industry:** Healthcare  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem
Branches over-ordered slow medicines and ran out of fast movers every month.

## What I did
Built per-SKU gradient-boosted forecasts with holiday and promotion features and a simple reorder-point report.

## Outcome
Stock-outs fell 31% and excess inventory fell 18% over two months of pilot.

**Skills:** Machine Learning, Data Science, Python, Statistics
