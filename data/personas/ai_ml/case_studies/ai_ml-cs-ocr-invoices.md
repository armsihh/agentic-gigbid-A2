# Invoice OCR and field extraction

**Client:** a logistics company in Rotterdam  
**Industry:** Logistics  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem
Accounts staff keyed 2,500 supplier invoices a month by hand.

## What I did
Combined OCR with a layout-aware extraction model for vendor, totals, VAT and line items, with confidence-based human review.

## Outcome
Field-level accuracy of 95%; manual keying time dropped by about 60%.

**Skills:** Computer Vision, Natural Language Processing, Python, Image Processing
