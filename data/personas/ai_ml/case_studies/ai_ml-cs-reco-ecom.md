# Product recommendation engine

**Client:** a Shopify fashion store  
**Industry:** E-commerce  
**Year:** 2022

## The problem
The store showed the same 'bestsellers' block to every visitor.

## What I did
Trained an implicit-feedback matrix factorisation model on 18 months of orders and served nightly recommendations through a cache.

## Outcome
Click-through on the recommendation block rose 2.3x; average order value up 9% in an A/B test.

**Skills:** Machine Learning, Python, Data Science
