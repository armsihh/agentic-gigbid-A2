# WhatsApp order-taking chatbot

**Client:** a Karachi bakery chain  
**Industry:** Food & Beverage  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem
Staff typed WhatsApp orders into a spreadsheet and lost orders at peak times.

## What I did
Built an LLM-driven bot that extracts items, quantities and delivery slots into structured orders, with a human handover button and a menu-grounded prompt.

## Outcome
Handled 63% of orders end to end in the first month; order errors dropped from 7% to under 2%.
