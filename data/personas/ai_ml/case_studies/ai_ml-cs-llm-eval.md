# LLM answer evaluation harness

**Client:** an Australian ed-tech company  
**Industry:** Education  
**Year:** 2025

## The problem
Prompt changes to a tutoring bot were shipped on gut feeling and sometimes broke answers.

## What I did
Built a regression harness with 400 graded questions, an LLM judge calibrated against teacher labels, and a CI gate on accuracy.

## Outcome
Judge agreement with teachers reached Cohen's kappa 0.71; two harmful prompt changes were caught before release.
