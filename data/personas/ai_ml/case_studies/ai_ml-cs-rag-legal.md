# Retrieval chatbot over legal contracts

**Client:** a Dubai-based legal-tech startup  
**Industry:** Legal  
**Year:** 2025

## The problem
Paralegals searched 9,000 PDF contracts by keyword and missed clauses phrased differently.

## What I did
Built a hybrid retrieval pipeline (keyword + embeddings) with clause-level chunking and citation of the source page in every answer.

## Outcome
Answer accuracy on a 120-question test set rose from 58% to 86%; every answer links to its clause.
