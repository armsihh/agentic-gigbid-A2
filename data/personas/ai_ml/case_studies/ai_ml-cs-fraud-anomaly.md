# Transaction anomaly detection

**Client:** a fintech wallet provider  
**Industry:** Fintech  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem
Fraud analysts reviewed transactions only after customer complaints.

## What I did
Built an isolation-forest plus rules pipeline scheduled hourly, with analyst feedback fed back into thresholds.

## Outcome
Caught 72% of confirmed fraud cases before complaints at a manageable 40 alerts/day.
