# Offline-first inventory PWA

**Client:** a hardware wholesaler in Lahore  
**Industry:** Retail  
**Year:** 2022

## The problem
Warehouse staff lost connectivity in the stockroom and counts were done on paper.

## What I did
Built a PWA that works offline, scans barcodes with the phone camera and syncs when back online.

## Outcome
Stock counts that took two days now take four hours.

**Skills:** React, Firebase, Web Application, JavaScript
