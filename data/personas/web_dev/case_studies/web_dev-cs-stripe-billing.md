# Subscription billing with Stripe

**Client:** a B2B analytics SaaS  
**Industry:** SaaS  
**Year:** 2024

## The problem
Invoices were created by hand and failed payments went unnoticed.

## What I did
Integrated Stripe Billing with plan tiers, proration, dunning emails and idempotent webhook handling.

## Outcome
Involuntary churn fell by a third; finance stopped manual invoicing entirely.
