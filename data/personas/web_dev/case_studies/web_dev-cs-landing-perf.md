# Marketing site rebuild for performance

**Client:** a Dubai real-estate agency  
**Industry:** Real Estate  
**Year:** 2023

## The problem
The WordPress site scored 31 on mobile PageSpeed and leads were dropping.

## What I did
Rebuilt as a statically generated Next.js site with a headless CMS and optimised images.

## Outcome
Mobile PageSpeed 31 to 94; contact-form leads up 41% in two months.
